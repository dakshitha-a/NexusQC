PySCF: full capability index
Source: summarized from the PySCF user guide, pyscf.org/user/
This is a written summary, not the manual text. PySCF's own site cannot be
crawled by this app (pyscf.org/robots.txt disallows the scraper), so these
summaries ship with NexusQC instead.

What PySCF can compute, by module. Use this to answer "can PySCF do X?" before
reaching for a specific method page. A capability appearing here means the
upstream package implements it; it does not mean NexusQC exposes it as a job
type. NexusQC's own job catalogue is the registry, not this file.

## Ground-state electronic structure

- pyscf.scf: Hartree-Fock. RHF, UHF, ROHF, GHF, and the density-functional
  counterparts RKS, UKS, ROKS, GKS in pyscf.dft. Second-order (Newton) SCF,
  stability analysis, level shifting, DIIS variants, and a range of initial
  guesses.
- pyscf.dft: Kohn-Sham DFT with LDA, GGA, meta-GGA, hybrid and range-separated
  functionals through libxc/xcfun. Dispersion corrections come from the dftd3
  and dispersion extension packages.
- pyscf.x2c: scalar and spin-orbit relativistic corrections (X2C, sfX2C1e).

## Correlated wave-function methods

- pyscf.mp: MP2, including spin-component-scaled variants and density-fitted
  MP2. See also pyscf.mp.dfmp2.
- pyscf.cc: coupled cluster. CCSD, CCSD(T), and EOM-CCSD for ionization,
  electron attachment and excitation energies. Restricted, unrestricted and
  generalized references.
- pyscf.ci: CISD, in restricted (RCISD), unrestricted (UCISD) and generalized
  (GCISD) forms, with unrelaxed density matrices and analytical gradients.
- pyscf.fci: full configuration interaction, with spin and point-group symmetry
  control, arbitrary-order reduced density matrices (1- through 4-particle) and
  transition density matrices.
- pyscf.mcscf: CASCI and CASSCF, state-specific and state-averaged, with
  external CI solvers (DMRG, selected CI, FCIQMC) pluggable in place of the
  default FCI solver.
- pyscf.mrpt: strongly contracted NEVPT2 on top of CASCI/CASSCF or DMRG.
- pyscf.mcpdft: MC-PDFT and multi-state variants including L-PDFT (ships in
  pyscf-forge).

## Excited states and spectroscopy

- pyscf.tdscf: TDHF and TDDFT, full response or Tamm-Dancoff, singlet or
  triplet, with oscillator strengths in length and velocity gauge, higher
  multipole transition moments, analytic excited-state gradients, and natural
  transition orbitals.
- pyscf.adc: algebraic diagrammatic construction, ADC(2), ADC(2)-X and ADC(3),
  for excitation energies (EE), ionization potentials (IP) and electron
  affinities (EA), including core-level IPs for XPS via core-valence
  separation.
- pyscf.gw: GW approximation for quasiparticle energies, with analytic
  continuation, contour deformation, or exact frequency integration.
- pyscf.agf2: auxiliary second-order Green's function theory, giving the whole
  charged-excitation spectrum at once.
- pyscf.pprpa: particle-particle RPA for ground and excited states.
- EOM-CCSD in pyscf.cc covers the same excitation types at coupled-cluster
  level.

## Structure, gradients and dynamics

- pyscf.grad: analytic nuclear gradients for HF, DFT, MP2, CCSD, CASSCF, TDDFT
  and more.
- pyscf.hessian and pyscf.hessian.thermo: analytic Hessians, harmonic
  vibrational analysis and thermochemistry.
- pyscf.geomopt: geometry optimization through geomeTRIC or berny, with
  constraints, custom energy/gradient callbacks, excited-state optimization and
  transition-state search. Quadratic steepest descent TS optimization lives in
  the qsdopt extension.
- pyscf.md: Born-Oppenheimer molecular dynamics, NVE (velocity Verlet) and NVT
  (Berendsen thermostat), on any method that supplies gradients.
- pyscf.eph: first-order electron-phonon coupling matrix elements, analytic
  (CPHF/CPKS) or by finite difference.

## Environment and embedding

- pyscf.qmmm: QM/MM electronic embedding, MM region as classical point charges
  entering the QM Hamiltonian, with analytic gradients.
- pyscf.solvent: implicit solvation. PCM family (C-PCM, IEF-PCM, SS(V)PE,
  COSMO), ddCOSMO, ddPCM, and SMD for solvation free energies; polarizable
  embedding through the CPPE library.

## Integrals, orbitals and performance

- pyscf.df: density fitting / resolution of identity for SCF and post-SCF, with
  automatic auxiliary basis selection and reusable Cholesky-decomposed
  integrals.
- pyscf.sgx: seminumerical exchange (chain-of-spheres style) for large systems
  and large basis sets.
- pyscf.lo: orbital localization. Boys, Pipek-Mezey, Edmiston-Ruedenberg,
  intrinsic bond orbitals, intrinsic atomic orbitals, meta-Lowdin and
  non-iterative Cholesky orbitals.
- pyscf.tools: cube file generation (cubegen), molden and fcidump export,
  Mulliken and other population analyses.

## Periodic systems

- pyscf.pbc.gto: crystals as a Cell object, built like a Mole plus lattice
  vectors and k-point meshes.
- pyscf.pbc.scf and pyscf.pbc.dft: periodic HF and DFT with Brillouin zone
  sampling.
- pyscf.pbc.df: periodic density fitting. GDF (Gaussian auxiliary), FFTDF
  (plane wave, also called GPW), MDF (mixed), and range-separated RSDF.
- Periodic counterparts exist for many correlated methods, including
  pyscf.pbc.cc, pyscf.pbc.ci, pyscf.pbc.tdscf, pyscf.pbc.gw and pyscf.pbc.eph.

## Extensions

Capabilities that live outside the core package and must be installed
separately, including DMRG solvers (dmrgscf with Block/block2/CheMPS2),
semistochastic heat-bath CI (shciscf), FCIQMC, dftd3 and dispersion
corrections, CPPE polarizable embedding, properties (NMR, EPR), pyqmc for
quantum Monte Carlo, qsdopt, and pyscf-forge which carries MC-PDFT. See
extensions_ecosystem.txt.
