PySCF: electron-phonon coupling and molecular dynamics (pyscf.eph, pyscf.md)
Source: summarized from the PySCF user guide, pyscf.org/user/eph.html, md.html
This is a written summary, not the manual text. PySCF's own site cannot be
crawled by this app (pyscf.org/robots.txt disallows the scraper), so these
summaries ship with NexusQC instead.

## Electron-phonon coupling (pyscf.eph)

The Born-Oppenheimer approximation fails when electronic states change rapidly
with nuclear position and approach each other in energy. Treating the moving
nuclei as a perturbation of the external potential, expanded to linear order
with quadratic nuclear motion about a minimum, gives the linear electron-phonon
coupling Hamiltonian. This is the standard model for coupled electron/nuclear
phenomena in the solid state.

PySCF computes first-order electron-phonon coupling matrix elements either
analytically through CPHF/CPKS or by finite differences. For periodic systems
the finite-difference implementation supports FFTDF-based Gamma-point
calculations only.

A geometrically relaxed structure is required for the theory to be consistent,
which is a precondition rather than a recommendation.

Mean-field references are supported, Hartree-Fock and DFT, restricted or
unrestricted. eph.EPH(mf) infers the right class from the reference.

Diagonalizing the mass-weighted Hessian can produce unphysical modes, either
imaginary or spuriously non-zero, arising from incompletely relaxed geometries,
insufficient quadrature accuracy, or numerical noise. PySCF filters modes below
80 cm^-1 by default, which also removes negative frequencies. The cutoff can be
changed at construction, and imaginary frequencies can be kept deliberately.

The coupling matrix is produced in the AO basis by default, or in the MO basis
on request.

## Molecular dynamics (pyscf.md)

Born-Oppenheimer molecular dynamics on any method that can supply gradients,
including CASSCF. Two ensembles:

- NVE, integrated with velocity Verlet.
- NVT, with a Berendsen thermostat only. Velocities are rescaled each step so
  the temperature deviation from the bath decays exponentially with a coupling
  time constant. The rescaling factor is clamped to the range 0.9 to 1.1.

An integrator takes a time step and a step count and can be constructed
directly from a method object. Running it writes two files, one of energies per
frame and one trajectory in xyz format.

Initial velocities can be supplied explicitly, which is how a run is restarted
or started at a chosen temperature, or sampled from a Maxwell-Boltzmann
distribution at a given temperature in Kelvin. The random seed is settable via
md.set_seed(). Accelerations use the most common isotope masses rather than
average isotopic masses.
