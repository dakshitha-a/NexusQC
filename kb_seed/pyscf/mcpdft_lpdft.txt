PySCF: MC-PDFT and L-PDFT (pyscf.mcpdft)
Source: summarized from the PySCF user guide, pyscf.org/user/mcpdft.html
This is a written summary, not the manual text. PySCF's own site cannot be
crawled by this app (pyscf.org/robots.txt disallows the scraper), so these
summaries ship with NexusQC instead.

## What it does

Multi-configuration pair-density functional theory computes the total energy
from a functional of the on-top pair density and the total electron density,
both taken from an MCSCF wave function. The on-top pair density is the
probability of finding two electrons at the same point in space, and the
non-classical part of the energy is called the on-top energy, the direct
analogue of exchange-correlation energy in Kohn-Sham DFT.

The point of the method: the pair density, unlike the spin density, can encode
strong correlation and spin symmetry at the same time. That is what lets
KS-DFT functionals be generalized from single determinants to multiconfigura-
tional wave functions.

Note that this module ships in pyscf-forge rather than the core package.

## An important convention

The orbital and CI coefficients are chosen to minimize the expectation value of
the ordinary molecular Hamiltonian in the underlying MCSCF wave function, NOT
the MC-PDFT energy itself. So an MC-PDFT object is also an MCSCF object, and
the MC-PDFT energy is evaluated after the fact.

A consequence worth knowing before interpreting results: with state-averaged
CASSCF or multi-root CASCI, each state's MC-PDFT energy is computed separately
and states keep the ordinal labels the underlying MCSCF gave them. State 2 can
therefore come out below state 1 in MC-PDFT energy.

## On-top functionals

The functional is named by a string passed as the second argument to the method
constructor.

- Translated functionals, prefix "t", generalize any pure LDA or GGA
  exchange-correlation functional, and meta-GGA functionals that depend on the
  kinetic energy density. Example: "tPBE".
- Fully translated functionals, prefix "ft", are available for pure LDA and GGA
  functionals. Example: "ftPBE".
- Prefixing a hybrid functional name does not work in general, because the
  literature contains several inequivalent definitions of a hybrid on-top
  functional. PBE0 is the exception, implemented as specified in the source
  reference. Custom global hybrids can still be constructed explicitly, for
  example a functional mixing a chosen fraction of CASSCF energy.

The quadrature grid is reachable through the object's grids attribute and can
be set at construction.

## Multi-state MC-PDFT and L-PDFT

MC-PDFT evaluates each state through a nonlinear functional expression, which
means potential energy surfaces for states of the same symmetry are not
guaranteed to cross only along a proper conical intersection seam. That is a
real problem for non-adiabatic dynamics.

Multi-state methods fix this by defining the state energies as eigenvalues of a
small effective Hamiltonian whose elements derive from the MC-PDFT energy
expression. In PySCF these are implemented as child classes of state-averaged
CASSCF.

L-PDFT is the preferred multi-state variant. It runs SA-CASSCF orbital
optimization first, then the L-PDFT step. Its first argument is a sequence of
state weights, exactly as for state_average_. The second argument selects which
multi-state flavour is used. L-PDFT alone also supports mixing states of
different point-group symmetries or spins.
